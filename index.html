Research-inspired approach
VOCast is inspired by research investigating wearable physiological signals—including heart rate, activity and photoplethysmography (PPG)—as potential digital biomarkers related to pain and vaso-occlusive episodes in Sickle Cell Disease. This prototype demonstrates personalized pattern analysis using simulated data.
VOCast — Research Prototype
VOCast is not intended to diagnose, predict with certainty, or replace professional medical care. Any risk pattern shown in this demonstration is simulated and should not be used for clinical decisions.
